How it works

1. Understand

The claim is cleaned, and its keywords, people, places, organisations and dates are extracted.

2. Match

The claim is compared with FEVER claims and evidence using TF-IDF and cosine similarity.

3. Classify

The closest match decides the result: Supported, Refuted or Insufficient Information.
